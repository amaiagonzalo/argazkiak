I'm Amaia Gonzalo, a photographer. I think of photographs the way a book does: one beside another, with silences and white space, until they tell a story.

I have published two photobooks. *Desde el punto de vista de las estrellas* is a work of visual poetry, presented in December 2024 at Montehermoso (Vitoria-Gasteiz). The second is *Cartografía de una despedida*.

Alongside the books, I also make yearbooks.
