---
title: Privacy and cookies
slug: privacy
---
This website does not use cookies or any other technology to store information on your device, and it does not track or analyse visits. The fonts are hosted on the website itself, so no third-party services are contacted while you browse.

It does not collect personal data either: there are no forms and no sign-up.

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any server, GitHub may log technical connection data, such as your IP address, for security purposes. You can read its [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
